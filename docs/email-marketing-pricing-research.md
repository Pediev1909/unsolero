# Email marketing pricing — research, 2026-08-20

The category was not published. This records why, and what was established, so
the work is not repeated.

## Why it did not ship

The five candidates price on **three different axes**, and no shared basis can
be stated honestly from here:

| Vendor | Prices by | Read at 1,000 contacts | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| MailerLite Comfort | subscribers | **$19 / month**, monthly billing | read from page |
| Zoho Campaigns Standard | contacts | **€5 / month** | read from page, EUR only |
| Brevo Starter | **emails sent**, not contacts | **€7 / month**, from 5,000 emails | read from page, EUR only |
| ActiveCampaign Starter | contacts | **$15 / month billed annually** | read from page; no monthly rate published |
| Kit Creator | subscribers | ~$39 / month | secondary sources only |

Two blockers, either of which alone is enough:

1. **Currency.** Zoho and Brevo serve EUR from a Bulgarian IP and do not honour
   a browser locale or an in-page currency control — the price is chosen
   server-side. The catalog is in USD. Publishing a EUR figure as USD is not a
   rounding error, it is a wrong number with a source attached to it.
2. **Basis.** Brevo charges for sends, the others for list size. Forcing all
   five onto one axis would produce exactly the false equivalence this site
   exists to refuse.

## What would unblock it

Someone on a US connection, or a VPN, opening each pricing page, setting it to
1,000 contacts and monthly billing, and reading the USD figure. Ten minutes of
human clicking. It is not an automation problem: the sliders yielded — the
MailerLite figure above was obtained by walking its slider index until the
label read 1,000 — but the currency does not.

Brevo stays on its own axis regardless, and its product page should say so.
"Priced by emails sent, not by list size" is the single most useful fact about
Brevo for a small business, and no comparison table that hides it is honest.

## Affiliate programmes for these, none of them in the PartnerStack marketplace

Recorded here because the category is worth building for the programmes behind
it, not only for the readers.

| Vendor | Terms | Apply |
|---|---|---|
| MailerLite | 30% recurring, **no 12-month cap** | mailerlite.com/affiliate |
| GetResponse | 33–35% recurring, or $100 one-off | getresponse.com/affiliate-programs |
| Kit | 30% for 24 months | kit.com/affiliate |
| ActiveCampaign | 20–30% recurring | activecampaign.com/partners/affiliate |
| Zoho Campaigns | 15% for 12 months, already approved | link `UCST` in the Zoho portal |
| Brevo | $5 per **free** account, $100 per paid | PartnerStack — locked until a first commission |

MailerLite is the best terms on this list by some distance: 30% for as long as
the customer pays, where almost everything else stops after a year.
