same links credit signups in both the EU and US data centres; reporting is
consolidated in the US portal (`referrals.zoho.com`).

they end up on product pages — so they live in the repository rather than in a
password manager.

## Terms that constrain how these are used

- 15% of Zoho's revenue for 12 months. Not perpetual.
- First product only. A referred customer who later buys a second product earns
  nothing on it.
- 90-day cookie. Sale must complete within 90 days of signup.
- The referral must stay a customer 60 days; commission accrues 90 days after
  the purchase.
- $100 minimum payout, PayPal or wire.
- Self-referral is prohibited, including family.
- Disclosure is required on any page carrying these links.
