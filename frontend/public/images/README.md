# Homepage demo imagery

The four `demo-*.webp` files are original AI-generated product mockups created for the fictional UNSOLERO seed catalog. They do not depict real commercial products. The source generations were resized to 1000 × 750 WebP files for responsive web delivery.

## Prompt set

All four images used this shared prompt direction:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: 4:3 homepage product card image for a fictional fitness equipment catalog. Premium, photorealistic luxury fitness product photography on a warm limestone-gray seamless studio floor and backdrop. Centered editorial composition with generous negative space, soft directional daylight, restrained shadows, and a charcoal, matte-black, warm-gray palette with subtle bronze details. The product must be fictional, unbranded, and physically plausible. No people, packaging, text, logos, watermark, neon color, dramatic smoke, gym clutter, excessive reflections, or science-fiction styling.

Subject variants:

- A compact pair of adjustable dumbbells with nested black steel plates and subtle bronze adjustment details.
- A compact foldaway flat workout bench in matte charcoal steel with refined black upholstery.
- A single adjustable cast-iron kettlebell with a broad black handle and compact selector system, without visible weight numerals.
- A neatly arranged five-piece resistance-band kit with black handles, fabric straps, a door anchor, and muted earth-tone tubes.
- A residential half rack with a compact footprint, pull-up bar, safety arms, and bronze adjustment pins.
- A compact Olympic-style barbell resting horizontally on two low-profile supports.
- A tidy stack of matte-black weight plates in graduated diameters with subtle bronze hub rings.
- A slim low-profile walking pad with a charcoal deck and minimal front display housing.

The four additional catalog subjects use the same prompt direction. Their source PNGs were resized to 1000 × 750 and optimized to WebP.
